## Summary

- Unified all human intervention requests into a single Escalations queue at /escalations (#432)
- Agent questions, redesign proposals (design decisions), and issue approvals (approval requests) now appear together in one centralized inbox
- Users can filter by status (Waiting / Resolved / All) and by escalation type (Blocker, Work Authorization, Question, Scope Expansion, Human Approval)
- Each escalation item links back to its source issue and supports direct action: quick resolve, custom response, approve/reject redesign, approve/reject approval
